---
title: Mira Life
---

Mira Life measures your personal capacity across six key dimensions to help you understand your true potential. By analyzing your daily habits, mood, and health metrics, it provides scientific, actionable insights to manage your energy without overwhelming you.

## Deliverables

- **Six-Dimension Tracking** — Monitors your emotional resilience, intellectual focus, recovery, connections, physical regulation, and inner drive.
- **Smart Metric Analysis** — Translates complex sleep patterns, tension, and heart rate data into clear insights about your daily state.
- **Adaptive Check-ins** — Uses simple morning and evening reflections to dynamically adjust your wellness plan based on how you feel.
- **Health Integrations** — Syncs seamlessly with Apple Health and Google Fit, or measures heart rate directly through your phone's camera.
